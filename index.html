Canvas OS – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Canvas OS is an Android home-screen launcher. This page explains what data the app accesses, why, and what happens to it. Canvas OS has no user accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

Data the app accesses

Approximate location

Canvas OS reads your device's approximate (coarse) location to show a current weather forecast on the home screen. Your coordinates are sent directly from your device to the Open-Meteo weather API to fetch the forecast. We do not operate Open-Meteo, do not store your location on any server of ours, and do not share it with anyone else. Open-Meteo's own privacy practices apply to that request; see their site for details.

Calendar

Canvas OS reads your device calendar (event title, time, and location) to show your next upcoming event on the home screen. This is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

Contacts

Canvas OS reads your device contacts so you can search for and call/message contacts directly from the home screen search bar. This is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted anywhere by us.

Search text you type

When you type two or more characters into the app search bar, that text is sent directly from your device to third-party search-suggestion services (Google, Bing, Brave, and/or DuckDuckGo, depending on your selected search engine) to show autocomplete suggestions. We do not operate these services and do not see or store this text ourselves. These providers may log queries according to their own privacy policies.

Data that stays on your device

Your home screen layout, app settings, and any backups you create are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to us. If your device's Android backup service is enabled, some of this data may be included in your device's own cloud backup, subject to your device manufacturer's and Google's backup policies — Canvas OS does not have its own server-side storage.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in is required or offered.
  • No advertising or ad SDKs.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We never sell your data.

Children's privacy

Canvas OS is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "last updated" date.
